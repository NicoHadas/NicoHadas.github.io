I am a computational biology PhD student at [the Francis Crick Institute](https://www.crick.ac.uk/), working in the [Fröhlich Lab](https://www.frohlichlab.com/), and an affiliated doctoral student with the [Ω Research Group](https://omega.cs.ucl.ac.uk/) at [UCL Computer Science](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/engineering/computer-science).

My research focuses on developing AI systems that combine mathematical modelling and scientific machine learning to expedite biomedical discovery.

I completed a Master of Science in Biomedical Informatics in Washington University School of Medicine's Department of [Informatics, Data Science & Biostatistics](https://i2db.wustl.edu/educationprograms/biomedical-informatics-and-data-science-internship/), where I conducted research in the [Horani Lab](https://horanilab.wustl.edu/) and the [Fuhai Li AI Lab](https://fuhailiailab.github.io/). I received my undergraduate degree in Biochemistry and Bioinformatics from the [University of Waterloo](https://uwaterloo.ca/).
