About

I am a computational biology PhD student at the Francis Crick Institute, working in the Fröhlich Lab, and an affiliated doctoral student with the Ω Research Group at UCL Computer Science.

My research focuses on developing AI systems that combine mathematical modelling and scientific machine learning to expedite biomedical discovery.

I completed a Master of Science in Biomedical Informatics in Washington University School of Medicine’s Department of Informatics, Data Science & Biostatistics, where I conducted research in the Horani Lab and the Fuhai Li AI Lab. I received my undergraduate degree in Biochemistry and Bioinformatics from the University of Waterloo.
